Questions hosts ask

I rent the flat. Can I list the spare room?

Yes. Most rooms here are listed by tenants whose flatmate is moving out. Just check that your rental agreement allows a new flatmate.

What does it cost?

Listing your room and answering requests is free.

Who sees my number and address?

Only the people you accept. Everyone else sees your first name, the room, the rent and how your home lives.

Can brokers use it?

No. One person can list at most two addresses, and anyone you accept can report a broker, which takes the listing down.
